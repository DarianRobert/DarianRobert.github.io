Machine learning research Honours team research · 2026

Smart Home Appliance Identification

Evaluating models beyond a headline score.

Our Honours research identifies ten known IoT device types from engineered network-traffic features, comparing neural models with a Random Forest baseline.

Research focus

The team study combines dataset auditing, model comparison, SHAP explanations and prepared-input inference profiling on x86_64 and ARM64.

  • Python
  • TensorFlow / Keras
  • scikit-learn
  • SHAP
  • LiteRT
Inside the research +

The workflow reconstructs a fixed training, validation and test split, fits preprocessing on training data, and selects neural models using validation evidence. It compares predictive performance alongside model size and inference cost.

A useful finding: the tuned Random Forest outperformed the selected neural models on held-out accuracy. The strongest neural score alone does not tell the whole story.

This is closed-set classification using engineered features. Profiling measures prepared-input inference, rather than a live packet-capture or end-to-end gateway system.
